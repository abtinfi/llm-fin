Single seed, greedy decoding. **Clean accuracy** is strict accuracy over all items (a deferral counts as wrong). **Model's own** is the decision parsed from the model's generation before the gate overrides it or UQ defers it. **CC** excludes control pairs. **Violation** is the share of UNSAFE items answered SAFE. † marks a row where the gate fired: there, clean accuracy and CC are partly an identity with the labelling rule, not a measurement. `cl` is the Aim 3 residual adapter h' = h + α·P_causal(h) (rank 32, layer 30, α = 1), trained on 1,000 train pairs; *unseen* restricts it to test pairs none of whose prompts it trained on.
